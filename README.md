# Self-Balanced Two-Wheel Robot

## Overview
A two-wheel robot designed to maintain vertical stability through optimized center of mass distribution rather than active feedback control.

## Design Principle
The stability of the robot is achieved by carefully positioning the center of mass relative to the wheel axis, ensuring mechanical balance without continuous tilt correction.

## Components Used
- Arduino Nano  
- L298N Motor Driver  
- DC Motors  
- Battery Pack  
- Custom Chassis  

## Concepts Applied
- Center of Mass  
- Mechanical Stability  
- Embedded C Programming  
- Motor Control  
- Hardware Debugging  

## Learning Outcome
Improved understanding of weight distribution, structural design, and practical circuit implementation.
